About Vishal

The only gap worth
closing

Your professional peers in native English-speaking markets aren't better than you. They just sound like it. That's the only gap we're closing.

Nearly a decade spent doing exactly that — working with professionals across banking, engineering, healthcare, law and beyond, helping them show up in English the way they already show up in their own language: with authority, fluency and presence.

I've also worked with clients specifically on British RP acquisition and pronunciation refinement.

I've taught myself Czech, Italian, Japanese and German — which means I understand what it actually takes to acquire a language as an adult. That insider perspective shapes how I teach. I'm not a monolingual trainer handing you a textbook. I know the grammar, the frustration, and the breakthroughs. That makes me a fundamentally different kind of coach.
